# misc
A repository of random FE000000 mods I've made. Feel free to import your saves from default fe000000!
(also the other dan-simon projects are still here right now feel free to play those on his website: https://dan-simon.github.io/misc
## Nerfing mods
- 92000000 is basically base FE000000 but all generator bonuses are ^0.9 (i.e 1e100 --> 1e90). Doesn't seem big until you realize 1e1e12 --> 1e9e11. Essentially it removes a tenth of the orders of magnitude.
